# Responsible disclosure

This case study is defensive and educational. Do not upload malware, original infection commands, secrets, account identifiers, raw personal logs or active download links. Keep original evidence under restricted access. Report suspected infrastructure through authorized provider or incident-reporting channels; do not probe or interfere with third-party systems without authorization.

Notify the owner privately if sensitive material is discovered. Redact before committing; deleting a file later does not remove its Git history.
